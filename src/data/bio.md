I am a Master of Computing (Computer Science) student at the [National University of Singapore](https://www.nus.edu.sg/) and a research intern in the [KISP Lab](https://kisp-nus.github.io/), working with [Prof. Prateek Saxena](https://www.comp.nus.edu.sg/~prateeks/). I received my B.Eng. in Computer Science and Technology from [Zhejiang University](https://www.zju.edu.cn/english/) in 2026.

My research lies at the intersection of **systems security**, **programming languages**, and **formal methods**. I want low-level software, such as Rust programs and the compilers and runtimes beneath them, to be memory-safe and verifiably correct. I currently work on runtime detection of memory-safety violations in unsafe Rust. Before that, I built an Isabelle/HOL backend for the [Aeneas](https://github.com/AeneasVerif/aeneas) Rust verification toolchain and evaluated it on components of the JIT compiler of sBPF, the virtual machine of the Solana blockchain.
